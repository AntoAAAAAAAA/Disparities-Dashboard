# Texas Minorities & Public Health Dashboard

An interactive Streamlit dashboard to explore disparities in food insecurity and SNAP eligibility across Texas, especially among Hispanic and Black populations. Built using county-level datasets to support data-driven public health work.

## Live App
**Try it here:** https://texasminoritiesdashboard.streamlit.app/

## Purpose
This project aims to:
- Highlight regional and racial/ethnic disparities in access to food and SNAP benefits
- Provide a clear, visual interface for exploring patterns in Texas public health data
- Support advocacy, community education, and research around food justice and equity

It was created as part of a public health research portfolio focused on data transparency and minority health.

## Features
- **Choropleth maps** of food deserts and SNAP eligibility across Texas
- **Bar charts & scatter plots** breaking down disparities by race/ethnicity
- **Rural vs. urban comparisons** of food access
- **Interactive filters** to explore specific variables
- **Key takeaways** and **personal reflections** from the analysis

## Why This Matters
Texas has large and growing Hispanic and Black communities who face systemic barriers to food security. By visualizing these disparities:
- Policymakers and advocates can better allocate resources
- Community organizations can use data to guide interventions
- Students and researchers can explore real-world public health data

## Built With
- **Streamlit** (frontend)
- **Pandas / GeoPandas** (data handling)
- **Plotly** (visualizations)
- **Python**
- Public datasets: USDA Food Atlas, Feeding America, Census data, and others
